Several factors are propelling the growth of the digital kitchen scale market. ## Restraints

Despite positive momentum, the market faces several restraints. One of the primary challenges is market saturation in developed regions, where most consumers already own digital kitchen scales. This saturation puts pressure on manufacturers to innovate and differentiate their products. Price sensitivity in developing economies can also hinder widespread adoption. Additionally, concerns over product durability and the reliance on batteries can dissuade environmentally conscious consumers, thereby slowing down potential market expansion.
